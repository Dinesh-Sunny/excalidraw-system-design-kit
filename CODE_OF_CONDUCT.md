# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, welcome newcomers, give and take feedback kindly, and keep discussions about the work. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not accepted in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

## Reporting

If someone breaks these rules, report it privately through GitHub's [report content](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) option or by messaging the maintainer, [@Dinesh-Sunny](https://github.com/Dinesh-Sunny). Reports are handled promptly and kept confidential. The maintainer may remove content or block people who don't follow these rules.
