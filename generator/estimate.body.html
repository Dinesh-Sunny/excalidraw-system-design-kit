    Why estimate at all

    Five minutes of rough maths decides most of the design. You are not chasing exact numbers, just the right size: thousands or millions, gigabytes or petabytes.

    How to use it

    Four moves, about a minute. Tap "Show me" to see each one on the page.

    How the numbers connect

    Every number comes from a few others and leads to a design choice. Tap any card to move along the chain.

    What if...

    Try a change and see which numbers move, by how much, and which stay put. Your own numbers stay as they are until you keep one.

    Say it in the interview

    Your numbers, as five short points. Reveal them one at a time while you talk, or show them all.
