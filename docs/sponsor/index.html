Questions

Who sees my logo?

Engineers practising system design and AI architecture: people opening the kit's site to find a block, and developers reading the README on GitHub.

Which logo do you show?

The GitHub organisation or website you give us when you pay, linked to your site with tracking tags so visits show in your analytics. It goes live within a day.

Do I need a GitHub account?

No. Pay by card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Link or US bank debit. GitHub Sponsors is there if your company prefers it.

Can my company get an invoice?

Yes. Add your VAT or tax ID at checkout and a receipt and invoice arrive by email with every payment.

Can we pay yearly?

Yes. Switch to yearly at checkout and get two months free.

Can I change tier or cancel?

Any time, from your billing page. Your spot stays until the end of the period you paid for. The full terms fit on one screen.

Will I see results?

Yes. You get a live stats page with logo views and clicks, plus a summary email on the 1st of each month.

Will the kit stay free?

Yes, MIT forever. No logos ever go inside the library file itself.
