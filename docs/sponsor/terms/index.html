Sponsor terms

Short and plain. If anything here is unclear, email us.

What you get

Each tier lists its spots on the sponsor page. Your logo or name goes live within a day of payment and stays up while your sponsorship is active. Company sponsors get a stats page and a results email on the 1st of each month.

Paying and cancelling

You pay monthly or yearly, in US dollars, by card, wallet or bank debit through Stripe, or through GitHub Sponsors. Stripe emails a receipt and invoice for every payment. Cancel or switch tiers any time from your billing page; your spot stays until the end of the period you paid for. Every spot we promise goes live, or you get your money back.

Featured

Featured is exclusive: one company at a time. If it is already booked, you show as Gold until it frees up, and we refund the difference.

Your logo and link

We use your GitHub organisation avatar or your website's icon, linked to the website you give us. To keep the kit a place engineers trust, we feature legal, honest products, and refund the unused time for any logo that falls outside that.

Privacy

At checkout we receive your email, name, company, website, GitHub name and payment details. Stripe or GitHub keeps the payment details; we never see your card. We use the rest only to place your logo or name and to email you about your sponsorship. Your email is never published or shared. Logo views and clicks are counted with GoatCounter, which sets no cookies and stores no personal data. Ask us to delete your details any time: email us or reply to any of our emails.

Contact

Email us about billing, invoices, your logo or your data. We're happy to help. Questions about the kit itself go to Discussions, anything to polish to Issues.
